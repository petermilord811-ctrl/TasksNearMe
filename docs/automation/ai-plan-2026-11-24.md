# AI features plan (2026-11-24)

The owner: "implement all ai feature to all possible features that can do what something special with ai." Built by helper H-AI in its own worktree; the coordinator lands it.

## What exists today

Every call goes through the balancer (`src/integrations/ai/balancer.ts`: Gemini, then Kimi, then Groq, free allowances only; Claude only when `AI_PROVIDERS` names it), asks for one zod-shaped answer, and masks people's text first (`asPromptText`: phones, emails, links, street addresses). Without a key, development and tests use rule-based stand-ins; production then hides the feature. The `ai_drafting` market flag is the one switch. Tests get the stand-ins through the `tnm_ai_stand_in` cookie (never in production).

## What this adds

Each one uses only the balancer, masks what it sends (`asPromptText`), treats the answer as untrusted plain text (length limits, contact details masked again), never acts for the person (drafts land in fields they edit and send themselves, labeled "with AI"), answers in the viewer's language, has a per-person limit, and hides or says so when AI is off. Results are `ActionResult`-shaped (`code: 'drafted'`), inputs zod-validated.

1. **What neighbors say** (helper's public profile). A few short points from the helper's published client reviews (3 or more with words), in English and Spanish, made by the job runner when the set of reviews changes and kept in `ai_results`, so the page never waits for a model. Special: a client gets the gist of every review in seconds, and the points come only from what neighbors wrote (no names, nothing invented, low ratings not hidden).
2. **Write my review with AI** (review form, both directions). From the stars, the chosen tags and a few words, a short, honest review of this booking in the reviewer's language. Special: more and better reviews, the trust signal the marketplace runs on; the draft follows the rating (a 2-star review stays a 2-star review, kind and factual).
3. **Get ready checklist** (booking hub, client and helper). "Make my checklist": 3 to 6 steps for the viewer's role from the agreed scope, the task's details, the category's rules (what it never includes), the time and whether it's remote; never the address. Kept per booking, role and language, so it's there on every visit; a booking whose scope changed offers a fresh one; ticks stay on the device. Special: the day goes right for both people (the space cleared, parts unboxed, the right tools, what to confirm).
4. **Price check** (posting, budget step). "Check my price with AI": AI reads the draft (words and details) and sizes the job (how many items or hours) and what drives the price; the app turns that into a range with the market's own numbers (the published price guide, and recent completed bookings in the category when there are 5 or more), and says whether the budget is under, within or over. Special: a fair price grounded in the city's real prices, never a number the model made up.
5. **Make it clearer** (Neighbors composer). Rewrites the post's title and words to be clear and kind, the author's meaning and voice kept, in their language, with Undo. Special: questions that get answered and fewer posts that land wrong.
6. **Draft an answer** (Neighbors post page, helpers). On a question or recommendation post in a service the helper offers, a helpful answer from the question and their own profile (services, tools), with no prices, promises or contact details. Special: helpers help their neighbors faster, which is how they're found.
7. **Case summary for staff** (admin case page). "Summarize with AI": what happened, what each side says, what's been done and what's still open, from what the case page already shows (never the restricted conversation). Staff tools copy is English only. Special: the owner runs support alone; a long dispute reads in a glance.
8. **Upgrade: profile drafts read reviews.** "Write it for me" on a helper's profile also gets what clients said in published reviews (masked), so the about-me can reflect it truthfully.

Not added: a plain-words search on Find helpers. The dashboard's AI search already turns words into the same filters, shows matching helpers and links to the filtered helpers page, and it's the plan-limited "AI search" product; a second box would either bypass those limits or duplicate their upgrade prompts.

## Shape

- Contracts: `src/contracts/ai-assist.ts` (inputs, views, `AiAssistResult<T>`), optional handlers on the existing screens' handler types; `ACTION_SUCCESS_CODES` gains `drafted`.
- AI: one file per feature in `src/integrations/ai/` (system text, prompt from masked facts, schema, tidy, stand-in, factory), like `offer-writer.ts`.
- Server: `src/modules/assist/` (`ai-results` table, the services, `actions.ts`), the review summaries job (`reviews.summaries`, in the background so the runner never waits on a model; the e2e build uses the stand-in).
- UI: the shared `AiDraftButton`/`AiDraftStatus` where it's a draft; small cards for the checklist, the price check, the summary and the case summary. Previews for every state.
- Tests: unit (prompts, tidy, masking), jsdom (each control), integration (services against the test DB with recording stand-ins), e2e (`ai-features.spec.ts`, both projects).
