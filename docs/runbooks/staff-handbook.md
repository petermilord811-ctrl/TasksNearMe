# Staff handbook

For the people who answer members, check helpers and keep TasksNearMe safe, from launch day on. Every step names the screen and the buttons exactly as they appear. The staff tools are in English.

For the launch itself (switching Gainesville on, who hears what), see [gainesville-launch.md](gainesville-launch.md). For deeper cases, see [dispute-handling.md](dispute-handling.md), [safety-escalation.md](safety-escalation.md), [payments-incident.md](payments-incident.md) and [reward-review.md](reward-review.md).

## Signing in

1. Sign in to TasksNearMe with your own staff account, the same way members do.
2. You arrive on **Enter your code**. Type the **6-digit code** from your authenticator app and press **Verify**.
   - The first time, you get a one-time setup code from an admin. Enter it as **Setup code**, press **Continue**, add TasksNearMe to your authenticator app, then press **Confirm and continue**.
3. You land on **Queues**. The menu at the side holds everything in this handbook.

Anyone who isn't staff, or who hasn't entered the code, sees "page not found" on every staff page.

## Time targets

| What | Target |
|---|---|
| ID photos | Decided within 24 hours of sending. The helper's screen says "usually within a day". The queue marks a row **Over 2 days** only after 48 hours: by then it's late. |
| Proof of address | Decided within 48 hours. The helper's screen says "a day or two". The queue marks anything older as **Over 2 days**. |
| Disputes and safety reports | First reply within 4 hours. Safety reports that describe danger follow [safety-escalation.md](safety-escalation.md) at once. |
| All other cases | First reply within 24 hours. |
| Every case | Resolved within 72 hours of opening. Nothing is due while the case waits on a member. |

In launch week, check **Queues** and **Verifications** at least every 2 hours between 8 am and 8 pm Eastern. Each case shows its target as **Due in…** or **Past due…**.

## 1. Checking helpers

Helpers can't make offers until our team has approved their ID photos and their proof of address. Your speed here is the time to a helper's first offer.

Open **Verifications**. Its tabs are **Proofs of address**, **ID checks** and **Background checks**, oldest first. Each row is a person: select their name to open their page, then work in the **Verification** section.

### 1a. ID photos

What you'll see in the queue: "ID photos to compare". Other ID rows need a different decision (see the end of this section).

1. On the person's page, under **Our ID photos**, press **Open the photos**.
2. Write why you're opening them in **Reason** (at least 10 characters), for example "Comparing the photos sent on Nov 21.", and press **Open**. Your name, the time and your reason go to the audit log ("Opened and logged"). The photos show: **The front of the ID**, **The back of the ID** and **The selfie**, with the selfie's instruction ("Selfie prompt"). The links work for 5 minutes ("These links work until…"); open them again if they run out.
3. Compare:
   - the face in the selfie with the photo on the ID;
   - the selfie follows its instruction (for example, a smile, or three fingers held up next to the face). The selfie isn't mirrored: "left" is the person's own left;
   - the ID is current (not expired) and every word is readable;
   - the person is 18 or older;
   - the name on the ID matches the account's name.
4. Press **Review**. Choose:
   - **Approve: on to the double check** when everything matches. The helper then takes the ID partner's double check.
   - **Ask for new photos** when something doesn't match. Choose **What the helper is told**:

| Choose | When | The helper reads |
|---|---|---|
| the ID is unreadable | blur, glare, a corner cut off | "We couldn't read your ID in the photos. Take them again in good light, with all four corners in view and no glare." |
| the selfie doesn't match the ID | not the same person, or the face isn't visible | "We couldn't match your selfie to the photo on your ID. Take new photos with your face clearly in view." |
| the selfie didn't follow its prompt | the instruction wasn't followed | "Your selfie didn't follow its instruction. Take new photos; you'll get a new instruction." |
| under 18 | the birth date shows under 18 | Their setup pauses: "Helpers need to be 18 or older." |
| the ID has expired | past its expiry date | "That ID has expired. Take photos of a current ID." |
| other | anything else | "We couldn't accept these photos. Take them again, or contact support if this keeps happening." |

   The list also has **the double check didn't confirm the ID** and **the ID is used on another account**. Those belong to the other ID rows at the end of this section, not to a first look.

5. Write **Note for the audit log** (at least 10 characters): what you compared and why, for example "Selfie and ID photo match; ID valid to 2029; name matches the account." The helper never sees your note, only the reason you chose.
6. Press **Approve** or **Ask for new photos**. You'll see "Updated.", and the photos show "Decided… by" your name (and "Turned down:" with the reason, if you asked for new ones).

The photos also show what the automatic reading found: "Name matches", "18 or older" and "Not expired", each yes, no or not sure. "Not sure" means you check it yourself. If **Approve** says "This isn't ready yet. Try again in a few minutes.", the reading hasn't finished; it takes up to 15 minutes after the photos arrive. If the reading "didn't answer in time", or the queue says "No automatic reading", approving skips the check for the same ID on another account, and the audit log says so: look extra carefully at the name and the photo.

Other ID rows, each with its own button in the **Verification** section and a note for the audit log:
- "The double check didn't confirm our approval": press **Let them try the double check again**, or **Turn the photos down** if the photos were wrong.
- "The same ID is on another account": press **It's in order** if both accounts are the same person's (for example, a closed old account), or **Turn down: not their ID**.
- "The ID's name differs from the account's": check the photos, then **Confirm the name**.
- "5 tries without a pass": **Allow new ID tries** when you're satisfied it's a genuine person.

### 1b. Proof of address

What you'll see in the queue: the kind of document and the neighborhood (for example "Utility bill · Near North Loop"), "Sent… ago", and either "Automatic reading: name, address and date match." or "Automatic reading: something to check." On the person's page, the reading shows as "Name matches", "Address matches" and "Within 90 days", each yes, no or not sure. It's a hint, never the decision: you still look at the document.

1. On the person's page, under **Home address**, press **Open the proof**, write a **Reason** and press **Open**. This is logged too. The address they typed shows as "Address entered:", with the document. The link works for 5 minutes.
2. Check:
   - the name matches the account;
   - the address matches the one entered ("Address entered:");
   - the document is dated within the last 90 days;
   - it's an accepted kind of document: a utility bill, a bank or insurance statement, a lease, a letter from a government agency, or a phone or internet bill;
   - it looks original, not edited or retyped.
3. Press **Review** and choose **Approve: the address is verified** or **Ask for another document**. If you ask for another, choose **What the helper is told**: unreadable, the name differs, the address differs, older than 90 days, not an accepted document, looks altered or not original, or other. Write the **Note for the audit log** (at least 10 characters, for example "Name, address and a date within 90 days all match.") and press **Approve** or **Ask for another document**. You'll see "Updated." The helper sees the reason you chose, never your note.

## 2. The support queue

Open **Queues** for the overview, or **Cases** for the list. **Cases** filters by **Queue** (**Disputes**, **Safety**, **Reports**, **Requests**), **Status** and **Assigned to me**.

What lands where:
- **Disputes**: problem reports, no-show reports, cancellation reviews.
- **Safety**: safety reports.
- **Reports**: reports about a person or about content, and "possible block evasion" flags (see section 4).
- **Requests**: payment, reward and account questions, and other requests.

### Working a case

1. Open the case and press **Assign to me**, so nobody else works it at the same time. A lead can take a case someone else holds.
2. Read the **Opening report**, the **Timeline**, the **People** and the **Booking**.
3. To answer, press **Reply**. Choose the person in **Send to** (only they receive it), write the **Reply message**, and press **Send reply**. To start from a prepared answer, pick one in **Start from a saved reply**; it replaces what you wrote.
4. To leave something for other staff, press **Add a note**. Only staff see notes.
5. When you're waiting for someone, press **Change status**, pick the **New status** and press **Save status**. The choices depend on where the case stands: **Waiting for client**, **Waiting for helper**, **With support** or **Escalated**. **Escalated** hands it to a lead.
6. To finish, press **Resolve**. Choose the **Resolution** and write a **Resolution summary** (10 to 1,000 characters). Everyone on the case you can write to reads it, never the person a report is about. Outcomes that move money show "What happens to the money" before you save.
7. After resolving, press **Close case**. A lead can **Reopen case** later.

Cases from the contact form, sent by someone who wasn't signed in, are answered by email (**Answer by email**). Never share account details by email: ask them to sign in.

### Reading a conversation

Messages between members are private. You may open a conversation only while a case about it is open, and only to work that case.

1. On the case, find **Reported conversation** and press **Open the conversation**.
2. Write **Reason for access** (10 to 500 characters), for example "Checking the messages the client reported on Nov 21.", and press **Log access and open**.
3. You see the messages as written, including contact details that were hidden from the other person. Your name, the time and your reason go to the audit log.

A booking's page has the same **Open the conversation** button, which works only while that booking has an open case.

## 3. Money

### Refunds

1. On the booking's page (from **Bookings**, or from a case), press **Refund**.
2. The dialog shows **Your limit**. Enter the **Refund amount (USD)** (any amount up to what's left, so part or all), choose **Goodwill** or **Fraud** as the **Refund reason**, and write a **Refund note** (10 to 1,000 characters, kept in the audit log).
3. Press **Record refund**. Above your limit the button reads **Ask for approval**, and a lead or finance approves it before the client is refunded.

Approving: a request shows under **Refund requests** on the booking. Press **Approve** or **Decline**, write a **Note** and press **Approve and refund** or **Decline the request**. You can never approve your own request, or act on a booking you're on.

### Disputes

A problem report or a no-show report is a case in **Disputes**. Resolve it with the outcome that fits:
- **Release in full**: the helper is paid the agreed price.
- **Settle at a lower price**: enter the **New task price (USD)**.
- **Refund in full**: the client gets everything back.
- **No further action**.

The money a resolution moves counts against your refund limit. See [dispute-handling.md](dispute-handling.md) for how to weigh evidence.

### No-shows

When a client reports that the helper didn't come, the app cancels the booking, refunds the client in full and gives the helper a "No-show" strike, without staff. If the helper disagrees, they contest it, and a high-priority **No-show report** case opens in **Disputes**. If they're right, reverse the strike (section 4) and resolve the case.

### A payment that's stuck

There's no button for a stuck payment. **Bookings**, filtered to **Needs attention**, shows "Payment failed". The app cancels a booking whose payment hold expires, and the client can book again. If a member says they were charged for a booking that was canceled, their message is a case in **Requests**. Reply that you're looking into it, add a note with the booking, and ask finance to look: finance and admins check **Payments** ("Needs a look") and, for a provider message that didn't arrive, **Webhooks**.

### Finance only

- Taking money back from a helper (**Take back from the helper**, which needs a case on the booking).
- Approving a refund request over $500.
- **Download the evidence pack** when a client's bank disputes a charge (admins too).
- The full **Payments** view and **Webhooks** (admins too).

## 4. Keeping the site safe

### Hiding content

- **A task**: open it from a case (**Open the task**). Under **Moderation**, use **Hide task**, **Close task**, **Remove a photo** or **Move to another category**, with a **Reason** (10 to 1,000 characters). A hidden task leaves the board, the feeds and its public page, and helpers can't send offers; the client still sees it. **Restore task** undoes a hide.
- **A community post or reply**: on its report case, **Open the post** or **Open the reply** takes you to it in the community. Use its **More** (⋮) menu there: **Hide**, **Remove** or **Restore**, with a reason.
- **A question or answer on a task**: from its report case (**Hide question**, **Remove question**, **Restore question**), or from its ⋮ menu on the task.
- **A review**: from its report case, **Hide review** (or **Show review again**), with a reason.

Hide rather than remove when you're not sure: hiding can be undone.

### Warnings

There's no warning button, and the staff tools never write to the person a report is about, which keeps the reporter safe.
- When you can write to the person on the case (the client or the helper of the booking, or whoever wrote in), a warning is a **Reply**: say what was wrong and what happens if it happens again. It stays on the case.
- For the person a report is about, act on what they did: hide the content, and suspend the account or request a ban when it's serious or keeps happening. In the resolution summary, tell the person who reported that you looked into it and acted, without details about the other account.

### Strikes

The app gives helpers strikes by itself, for a late cancellation or a no-show. There's no button to add one. To take one back after an appeal, open the helper's page, find **Strikes**, press **Reverse strike** and explain the outcome.

### Suspending and banning

On the person's page, under **Account**:
- **Suspend account** (a lead, trust & safety or an admin): they're signed out everywhere, can't sign in, and their helper profile is suspended. **Reinstate account** undoes it.
- **Suspend helper**: they can still use the app as a client, but can't help.
- **Request a ban** (trust & safety or an admin). A ban needs a second reviewer: another trust and safety member or an admin approves it with **Approve the ban** before it takes effect. You can't approve your own request. **Decline the ban** cancels it (another reviewer, or the person who asked).
- Only an admin can lift a ban.

Each asks for a **Reason**, kept in the audit log. Staff accounts have no suspend or ban buttons: an admin removes their roles first.

**What a ban also does:** any card a banned account used is refused to every other account. Someone who comes back with a new email and the same card is told "This card can't be used here", and never why. Reinstating the account lifts it.

### Block evasion

When someone reaches a person who had blocked another account that used the same card, the app opens a **Reports** case: "Possible block evasion". It's a flag for review only: nothing was done to either account, and two people can share a card (a household).
1. Read who reached whom and how (message, offer, request or question). If a message raised it, **Reported conversation** lets you read it, with a reason.
2. If it's the same person avoiding a block, suspend or request a ban, and say so in the resolution.
3. If not (a family card), resolve with **No further action**.
These cases have no one to reply to: the flag never tells either person.

### "Not you?": someone else changed an account

Every notice about a new email, phone or way to sign in has a "Not you?" link. When the owner uses it, the app signs out every device, holds the account's email, phone and ways to sign in, and opens an urgent case in **Requests**. For a new way to sign in, it also removes every way to sign in except a code sent by email. The person's page says "Email, phone and sign-in changes held since…".
1. Work the case with the owner: what changed that they didn't do, and whether anything else looks wrong (bookings, messages, payouts).
2. Once the account is back with its owner, trust & safety or an admin opens the person's page and, under **Account**, presses **Clear the hold**, then **Clear hold**. They can then change their email and phone and add ways to sign in again. Nobody clears a hold on their own account.

## 5. What staff never do

- **Never look someone up without a case or a task in front of you.** The search at the top (**Search people**, by name or email) and the **People** page open any account. The app doesn't ask why, but every person's page you open goes in the audit log with your name and the time. Opening photos, proofs, addresses and conversations also asks for a reason.
- **Never share anyone's contact details**, address or messages with another member, by email, phone or any other way. Members reach each other only through the app.
- **Never act on your own account or bookings**, or on a case you're part of. The app hides your own cases and refuses most of these actions, but not every one: don't try.
- **Never ask a member for a password or a code.** We never need them.
- **Never promise an outcome before it's decided**: "we're looking into it" until the resolution.
- **Never write a reason or a note you wouldn't want the member to read** in a dispute. Notes stay internal, but they're evidence.

## Where everything is recorded

Every staff action, and every look at restricted information, writes a line to the **Audit log** with your name, the time, what you did, to whom, and your reason. Leads and admins read it under **Audit**, newest first. To find something, fill in **Who** (a name, an email or an id), **Action**, **Target type**, **Target id**, **From** and **To**, then press **Apply filters**. Actions show by their names in the log, for example `identity_capture.viewed` when someone opened ID photos and `address_proof.viewed` for a proof of address. Lines can't be changed or deleted in the app.

What it records includes:
- opening a person's page;
- opening ID photos, proofs, an address or a conversation;
- every verification decision;
- replies, notes, status changes, resolutions, closing and reopening cases;
- refunds and approvals;
- hiding and restoring content;
- reversing strikes;
- suspensions, bans and reinstatements, and clearing a hold;
- changes to staff roles.
