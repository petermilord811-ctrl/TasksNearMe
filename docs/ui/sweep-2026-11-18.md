### Notes, not counted as defects

- `/sign-up` and `/help/contact` log 8 console errors each from the Turnstile iframe: the CSP blocks its `data:` connect test, and a sandboxed `about:blank` frame logs too. Worth one check that the widget passes in a real browser (37).
- The public top bar is a `div`, so the site has no banner landmark. The app bar is the same.
- The e2e build's header avatar once came back with an absolute `http://localhost:3300/api/files/…` src instead of a relative one (desktop account-contact photo, flaky, 9.3).
- Anonymous visitors on the live site see no tasks or helpers, since the demo marketplace shows only to signed-in testers. So the public task and helper pages couldn't be checked signed out.
- 404 pages for addresses with a dot in them (`/wp-login.php`) never reach the app: src/proxy.ts skips them, and Next's plain 404 answers. That's intended, since it's cheap for bots.

## Checked and found correct

- **Billing:** rebate math and caps, once per booking, the monthly cap under concurrent completions, month boundaries in the home-market zone, webhook signatures and dedupe, permissions, and plans in the data export and deletion.
- **Dashboard:**
  - URL filters survive a reload, and tab counts match.
  - "Needs you" items aren't counted twice.
  - The exact address appears only in `ExactAddressPanel`.
  - Money comes from the server's breakdowns, and the (flow) move kept every URL.
- **Portal:**
  - No permission, IDOR or link-integrity problems.
  - Blocks apply both ways in the stream, counts, Saved, rail, stats and reply previews.
  - The SQL for Hot, New and Top and for the facets agrees with what's shown.
- **Getting in:**
  - Sign-up dedupe gives the same response for an existing email, and the signed cookie is safe.
  - Google and Microsoft follow the email rules.
  - The phone off, asked and required states are consistent everywhere else.
  - Photo type, size, re-encoding, visibility and ownership are checked.
