# UI-033 handoff: admin bookings, task moderation, audit log

Built by **Claude** on `main` (Codex is at its usage limit; `docs/automation/decisions.md`).

## 1. Built
- `src/ui/screens/admin/admin-bookings-screen.tsx` (`AdminBookingsScreen`): tabs with counts (links that keep the search), a GET search form, and the rows (a table on wide screens, cards on phones) with the status, the people (linked to their staff pages), the start, what the client paid, and the flags.
- `src/ui/screens/admin/admin-task-screen.tsx` (`AdminTaskScreen`, `AdminTaskContent`) and `admin-task-controls.tsx` (`AdminTaskControls`):
  - the screen shows the task's facts, the hidden notice (who, when, why), the poster with a link to their account, the booking link, the description, the details, the photos and the cases;
  - the controls show the supplied actions only, each confirmed in a dialog with a required reason (and a photo or a category).
- `src/ui/screens/admin/admin-audit-screen.tsx` (`AdminAuditScreen`): a GET filter form (who, action family, target type, target id, dates), the entries (when, who with their role, the action, the linked target, the reason, the details), and "Older entries".
- `src/ui/screens/admin/admin-case-rows.tsx` (`AdminCaseRows`): the case rows from the people page, shared with the task page.
- "Open the task" links on the staff booking page and the case page.
- Previews (17):
  - `screen-admin-bookings/{attention,upcoming,search,empty}`;
  - `screen-admin-task/{open,hidden,booked,read-only,hide-dialog,remove-photo-dialog,recategorize-dialog,saved,validation-failed,not-allowed}`;
  - `screen-admin-audit/{default,filtered,empty}`.
- Tests: `admin-ops-screens.test.tsx` (copy for every filter, flag and action; GET forms on the route builders; only supplied actions; a reason is required; the photo and category are sent).
