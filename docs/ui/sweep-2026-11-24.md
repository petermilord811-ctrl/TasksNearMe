# Whole-platform sweep, 2026-11-24

The owner asked on 2026-11-24: "Deep check and fix uncomfortable UIs and logics about whole platform."
tasksnearme-a4 ran this pass and keeps this document. tasksnearme-5b took Neighbors and the task lists the same day
(docs/ui/sweep-2026-11-24-neighbors.md). tasksnearme-aa took the findings in its own fresh code: Team up and family
followers. The pass covers what changed since the last sweep (docs/ui/sweep-2026-11-18.md, closed): 89 commits from
2026-11-21 to 11-24, among them the no-fee payment modes, plans as a ladder, the chat rounds, quiet hours and thank-you
notes, Sparks milestones, neighborhood days, family followers, building groups and Team up.

## What was checked

| Area | How | By |
|---|---|---|
| Every screen, signed out and by role | A crawl of the dev server at 360 × 780 (touch) and 1280 × 800, signed out and as a client (Maya), a helper (Jordan), a helper setting up (Priya), staff (Morgan) and a Spanish client (Ana). It recorded status, redirects, console and page errors, failed requests, sideways scroll, elements past the edge, raw copy keys and load time. Its screenshots were read on contact sheets, phone first. | a4 |
| Money and payment modes | Read-only review: paid directly vs held, the hold fee, plans, refunds, disputes, receipts, staff money views, Team up's and neighborhood days' money. | a4's reviewer |
| Messages, the chat window and notifications | Read-only review: read state, quiet hours, cards, masking, followers' and Team up's conversations. Fixed by a4's messaging fork. | a4's reviewer |
| Accounts, verification, Sparks and privacy | Read-only review: the 11-23 helping rule, Sparks' contact gate, thank-you notes, the new tables in the export, deletion and retention. Fixed by a4's accounts fork. | a4's reviewer |
| The booking lifecycle and route dialogs | Read-only review: Team up, family followers, neighborhood days and the day-of chips against booking states, and every dialog route. | a4's reviewer |

The crawl found no error screens, raw keys or broken pages. It found one 2 px sideways scroll (V5). The staff pages that
"crashed" at 1280, and the Spanish client's failed sign-in, came from the dev server restarting under memory mid-crawl
(refused connections), not from the app.
