# UI audit: where the app isn't comfortable (2026-11-18, plan 9.1)

The owner: "Now, it's time to find uncomfortable UIs - beside focus on UI, real comfortable is broken." This audit walks every main page signed out, as a client and as a helper, at 360 and 1280 px, in light and dark, with Spanish spot checks, and routes each finding to the Part 9 row that owns it.

## Summary

93 findings: 1 critical, 9 high, 51 medium, 32 low. What makes the app feel broken, most important first:

1. **Photos were lost on the live preview (C1).** Every upload on tasksnearme.com was written into the release folder and deleted at the next deploy, so the owner's avatar became an empty circle in the header, on the account page and on cards. A photo also showed in only a few places (H1), with no way to position it (M2). Fixed in 9.3.
2. **Pages jump after they look loaded.** On desktop, content that arrives late pushes the footer or the hero (CLS 0.69 on a category page, 0.68 on a helper profile, 0.37 on the waitlist, 0.25 on the landing: H2–H4, M5). The logo is blurred for 2–3 s on every full load and replays its entrance on page switches (M3, M4). Owner: tasksnearme-f7 (9.4, 9.5).
3. **Lists are walls of tall cards.** Tasks, bookings, receipts and offers are 200–360 px cards on phones, so pages run 3,700–6,000 px. The dashboard repeats the whole task list (M15), My tasks shows open tasks twice (M27), and a booking states its price four times (M29). Settings pages are the longest: the account page is 5,800 px (M37), and the helper profile editor is 10,800 px (H7). Owners: 9.9 and 9.14-app (9c's helpers), with H7 under 9.11.
4. **Phone chrome eats the screen.** The header, the mode switch and the tab bar take about 180 px of an 800 px phone on every signed-in page (M38). Detail pages add a third sticky bar (M30), and in a chat the message box starts under the tab bar (M40). Owner: 9.14-app.
5. **Dead ends and contradictions.** Expired offers can't be renewed or replaced (M21, M31), closed chats don't say why (L13), job hubs send helpers to "Post a task" (M42), the cities page says a prelaunch city is open (H8), and the waitlist offers Austin while Austin is paused (M48). Owners: 9.10, 9.14-app, 9.14-public.
6. **The background fights the content.** Marble veins run behind paragraphs and headings (M11, M12), the become-a-helper page uses four palettes with olive cards in dark (M13), empty states carry stain-like glows (L6, L7), and option rows lose their borders in dark (M14). Owner: tasksnearme-f7 (9.8).
7. **Ways in are missing or hidden.** There is no sign-up page (H5), sign-in hides in the phone menu (M46), and Find helpers opens with a form instead of helpers (M35). Owners: tasksnearme-37 (9.6, 9.14-public) and 9.14-app.
8. **The landing is long and busy on phones.** It runs 15,000 px with whole sections that appear only once scrolled to (M7). Its first controls are cramped (M6, M10, L5), its map controls crowd the map (M9), and the prelaunch map reads as empty (M8). Owner: tasksnearme-f7 (9.7).
9. **Copy promises things the product no longer asks for.** Public pages still say helpers verify their phone (M1, after 9.2). A raw message key showed on the availability page (H9, fixed here).
10. **Helper setup is nine pages** (H6). There is no photo step, no help without Claude, and typed text is lost on Back. Rebuilt in 9.11 as one page in three parts.

**Fixed in this audit:** H9, the availability page's raw `availability.marketTime` key (the zone wasn't passed); M50, the gift card's fine print at 4.49:1 contrast (now the full color); L27, the bookings tabs wrapping at 1280. C1, H1 and M2 are fixed in 9.3, and H6 in 9.11. The other "quick fix" items are small bugs with no Part 9 row, left for the coordinator to place.

**Done well:** dark mode holds up across nearly every page. Cards and rows read cleanly on both backgrounds, with the exceptions listed under 9.8. No page scrolls sideways at 360, axe found one serious issue (M50), and no page switch showed a blank flash.

- **What was looked at:** every sheet, by eye. Screenshots are in `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/` (not committed): `shots/<group>/<page>--<width>-<theme>.png`, side-by-side review sheets in `review/`, the measurements in `notes/<group>.json`.

Maya's lists carry many leftover e2e tasks ("Keyboard bookshelf …"). The seeded offers on her desk task have expired. Dev-mode timings are slower than production; a page that "feels slow" here was checked against its skeleton and shift numbers rather than raw load time.
- **The landing's lower sections** fade in only once scrolled to, so the full-page shots show them blank (that behavior is M7). They were captured screen by screen afterwards (`shots/landing-scroll/`, 03:20 UTC); by then the header had gained Sign in and Sign up and the footer had become compact disclosures, so H5's header half, M45 and M46 were already being addressed. ## Critical: broken, or private data at risk (1)

### C1 · account, header, helper profile (deployed preview) (9.3)

- **Where:** `https://tasksnearme.com/app/account` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/avatar/2-reload-1280-light.png`
- **What:** A profile photo set on the deployed preview disappears at the next deploy: the owner's photo (uploaded 2026-11-14) now answers 404 at /api/files/<id>, so the header, the account page and every card show an empty peach circle. Cause: the standalone build traced the build machine's .data/uploads into the bundle, so each release ships a real .data/uploads folder and deploy.sh's link to the lasting uploads folder lands inside it; uploads are then written into the release and deleted with it. The same trace copies any local uploads (ID photos, proofs of address, data exports) into build output.
- **Why it matters:** The owner's 'avatar set broke' report: every photo (and task photo, receipt or ID capture) uploaded on the preview is lost at the next deploy, and a broken image is what people see.
- **Fix:** Done in 9.3: the storage folder resolves at runtime only (no tracing), bundle.sh drops .data, deploy.sh replaces any .data/uploads folder with the link (re-run setup-vps.sh to install it), and a photo that can't load shows the initials.

## High: clearly uncomfortable on a main path (9)

### H1 · messages, bookings, my tasks, my clients, reviews (9.3)

- **Where:** `/app/messages` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/client/messages--360-light.png`
- **What:** A profile photo shows only on the account page, the account menu, the helper editor, the helper's public profile and helper cards. Everywhere else a person appears in the app (inbox and thread headers, bookings and the booking hub, my tasks' newest offer, my clients, invites, the review page, rebooking) shows initials even when the person has a photo.
- **Why it matters:** People recognize who they're talking to and booking by face; a photo that shows in two places feels broken.
- **Fix:** Done in 9.3 for the app's own pages: those summaries carry the photo and the screens pass it. Community authors come with 9.10 (its contract already adds the photo).

### H2 · category-furniture (9.4)

- **Where:** `/gainesville/furniture-assembly` · 1280 px · dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/anon/category-furniture--1280-dark.png`
- **What:** When the page data arrives late, the main content pops in and pushes the footer (already on screen) down. Measured CLS 0.689 at 8.8 s; the shifting nodes are the footer and its horizon glow div. Same on helper-profile-jordan (0.684 at 3.8 s) and jobhub-furniture (0.237 at 5.2 s), in the slower 1280 runs.
- **Why it matters:** The page visibly jumps after it looks loaded; anyone already reading or reaching for a footer link loses their place.
- **Fix:** Give these routes a Suspense fallback (skeleton) as tall as the real main column, or keep the footer out of the first paint until main resolves (footer inside the content boundary / main min-height of the viewport).

### H3 · landing (9.5)

- **Where:** `/` · 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/anon/landing--1280-light.png`
- **What:** The hero shifts about 1 s after load in both themes (CLS 0.249 at 989 ms; node: the hero's absolute backdrop div). Something in the hero (town still or canvas, pill row, assistant) changes size after first paint.
- **Why it matters:** This is the first impression page, and the headline and search move under the user's eyes and cursor.
- **Fix:** Reserve the town and assistant boxes with a fixed aspect-ratio or min-height from the first paint, and don't mount extra hero rows after hydration.

### H4 · waitlist (9.5)

- **Where:** `/waitlist` · 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/anon/waitlist--1280-light.png`
- **What:** Layout shift of 0.366 about 0.55 s after load. The shifting nodes are the footer and its glow, so content above the footer changes height after hydration. Sign-in has the same pattern at 0.148.
- **Why it matters:** At 1280x900 the footer is in the first screen, so the page visibly jumps under the cursor right after it appears. 0.366 is in Google's 'poor' range.
- **Fix:** Find the client-only block above the footer and render it on the server at its final height, or reserve its space with a min-height. Add a CLS check of public pages at 1280 to the e2e suite.

### H5 · sign-up (9.6)

- **Where:** `/sign-up` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/anon/sign-up--360-light.png`
- **What:** /sign-up lands on /sign-in, which shows 'Sign in or create an account' and the footnote 'Sign in and sign up happen here, with the same steps.' There is no sign-up page; the desktop header offers only 'Sign in'.
- **Why it matters:** The owner named the missing sign-up page. A new visitor sees no clear 'create an account' entry, and links to /sign-up show a sign-in screen.
- **Fix:** Build the 9.6 /sign-up page (name, email, what you're here for, Google and Microsoft), link it from sign-in and the header, and keep /sign-in for returning people.

### H6 · helper setup (onboarding profile, services, area) (9.11)

- **Where:** `/app/helper/onboarding/profile` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `docs/ui/profile-setup-9.11.md (§1; the shots in artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/draft/ already show the new flow)`
- **What:** Helper setup asks for the profile on three separate pages (profile, services, area) with a server round trip each; text typed and not saved is lost on Back or a reload; the headline and 'about me' come first, before the services and area a good headline names; there is no photo step; without Claude in production 'Write it for me' is hidden and the helper faces two empty boxes; the first page shows all nine steps; prices start at the category minimum.
- **Why it matters:** This is where new helpers give up: the owner asked for 'a more comfortable profile setup method'.
- **Fix:** Done in 9.11: one page in three parts (What you do, Where you help, Introduce yourself) with autosave, Back and Next that keep everything, the photo with its adjust step, a live preview of the card, Claude's draft or written examples, and prices prefilled from the typical range.

### H7 · helper-profile (9.11)

- **Where:** `/app/helper/profile` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/helper/helper-profile--360-light.png`
- **What:** The helper profile editor is 10,775 px on a phone (about 13 screens): every section is open at once (Profile, Services with each service a ~900 px fieldset, Area, Tools, Work samples, Verification, Visibility), each with its own 'Save changes' (five in all). Before any of it come a finished gift card ('3 of 3 checks done') and a 'Your verification: 5 of 5 done' checklist, which repeat each other and the Verification section; at 1280 those two panels push the editor's tabs below the first screen (they start at about 1,100 px).
- **Why it matters:** An active helper opens this page to change a price or a photo and has to scroll past completed checks and every form to find it.
- **Fix:** Show sections as a list of summary rows (e.g. 'Services: Furniture assembly from $45, Moving help $35/h' with Edit) that open one at a time, with one save per open section; once all checks are done, collapse the gift card and the checklist into one 'All checks done' line (or move them under Verification).

### H8 · cities (9.14-public)

- **Where:** `/cities` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/anon/cities--360-light.png`
- **What:** The page contradicts itself. 'Where we are' lists Austin and Gainesville as 'Opening soon', but 'Every state' says Texas '1 city open' and Florida '1 city open'. The Florida page repeats it, and the footer calls Gainesville 'First city'. The state counts include prelaunch markets.

## Medium: inconsistent, cramped or awkward, but usable (51)

### M1 · become-a-helper (9.2)

- **Where:** `/become-a-helper` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/anon/become-a-helper--360-light.png`
- **What:** Public copy treats phone verification as required: the gift card's step 1 'Verify your phone', help 'Getting verified as a helper - Phone, ID and home address checks', the help article 'Every helper verifies their phone', and the 'Phone verified' badge on trust-and-safety.

### M2 · account (9.3)

- **Where:** `/app/account` · 360 and 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/avatar/1-set-1280-light.png`
- **What:** Choosing a photo uploads it at once and the server crops a square around what it guesses is the subject; there is no preview or way to position it, and the header picks the new photo up only after the page's refresh round trip (about 1-2 s).
- **Why it matters:** Group shots and wide photos crop wrongly, and people can't see what others will see before it's saved.
- **Fix:** Done in 9.3: an 'Adjust your photo' step (drag or arrow keys to move, slider or +/- to zoom, the round frame shown), the adjusted photo shown at once while it uploads.

### M3 · landing (9.5)

- **Where:** `/` · 1280 px · light and dark · `artifacts/ui-audit-2026-11-18/shots/anon/landing--1280-dark.png`
- **What:** In every 1280 capture (all pages, both themes), taken about 2.5 s after load, the header logo (mark and wordmark) is still blurred and half-transparent; in dark mode it is almost invisible. A viewport-only shot at 3 s showed it crisp, so the bloom-in/enter entrance seems to start late (likely at hydration). The phone mark is crisp. Transition frames show it re-blurring on page switches too.
- **Why it matters:** For 2-3 s on every full desktop load the brand mark looks out of focus or broken, next to sharp nav text.
- **Fix:** Don't blur the logo: a short fade of at most 300 ms or none at all, played from CSS at first paint and never restarted by hydration or a remount. Check in a production build.

### M4 · transition-landing-to-pricing (9.5)
