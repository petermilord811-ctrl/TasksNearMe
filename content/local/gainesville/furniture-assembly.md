Most flat-pack furniture in Gainesville arrives in boxes from online stores, or comes from IKEA's stores in Orlando and Jacksonville, a couple of hours' drive away. Whatever the brand, the job is priced per item, so say what you have: the brand, the model and how many boxes. A desk in one box and a wardrobe in six are different jobs.

## Apartments near campus and older homes

Many people near the University of Florida live in apartment buildings. Tell your helper the floor, whether there's an elevator, and where to park; some buildings ask you to book the elevator or the loading area for bulky deliveries. In older homes, like many in Duckpond or the Pleasant Street district, mention narrow doorways and stairs, so each piece is built in the room where it will stay.

## Move weeks

New furniture comes with UF's move weeks, in August before fall classes and when semesters end in December and May. If you're moving in then, post early so you have time to compare offers.

## Anchoring to the wall

Tall dressers and bookcases should be anchored against tipping. If you want that, include it in your task: it's part of the job only when it's agreed. Anchors need studs or the right wall plugs, so say whether your walls are drywall, plaster or concrete block. Mounting a TV isn't furniture assembly.

## Before your helper arrives

- Put the boxes in the room where the furniture goes, with space to work around them.
- Keep the hardware bags and the instructions together.
- If you rent, check your lease before anything is drilled into a wall.
