Computer and phone help covers device setup, Wi-Fi and printers, backups and basic cleanup, and patient how-to help, at home or in a guided screen-sharing session. A helper never handles your passwords or financial accounts, and unattended remote access isn't allowed.

## New devices, new semesters

The start of a semester is when many people in Gainesville set up a new laptop, phone or printer, or move photos and files from an old one. Say which devices you have, their make and model if you know them, and what you'd like to end up with.

## Wi-Fi in older and longer homes

Plaster walls in older houses, like many near downtown, and long single-story homes can leave rooms with a weak signal. A helper can look at where your router sits and suggest a better spot or a mesh system; buying new equipment is up to you.

## At home or on screen

Quick questions often fit a guided 30- or 60-minute session where you share your screen. Setting up a printer, a TV or Wi-Fi usually needs a visit. Have your devices charged and nearby, and your Wi-Fi name at hand; you type any passwords yourself.
