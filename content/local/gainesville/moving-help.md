Moving help covers the lifting: loading and unloading, carrying, moving furniture between rooms, and packing, by the hour. You arrange the truck; helpers don't carry your things in their own vehicle.

## Around UF's move weeks

Thousands of students move in Gainesville at the same time: in August before fall classes, and when semesters end in December and May. Rental trucks and help get booked up, so post your task early and keep your window flexible if you can.

## Heat and summer storms

From June to September, mornings are cooler and afternoon thunderstorms are common. Plan the heavy work early, keep moving blankets or plastic wrap ready for furniture and mattresses, and say if there's a covered spot near where the truck will park.

## Before your helper arrives

- Reserve the truck, and the elevator or loading area if your building asks you to.
- Have boxes packed and labeled, or say if packing is part of the task.
- Measure big pieces and tight doorways or stairs.
- Say how many hours you expect, and the most hours you want to pay for.
