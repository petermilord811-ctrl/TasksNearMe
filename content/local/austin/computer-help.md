Computer and phone help is for getting comfortable with your devices: setting up a new laptop or phone, getting Wi-Fi and printers working, backups and a basic cleanup, and patient how-to help. It happens at home or in a guided screen-sharing session.

## At home or remote

Wi-Fi and printer problems are often easier in person, because the helper can see the router and the printer. How-to questions and software setup work well remotely. With screen sharing you stay in control of your device and type your own passwords: a helper never asks for them and never installs remote-control tools.

## Booking for a parent or grandparent

Say who will be there and what they'd like to do on their own afterwards: video calls with family, bigger text, sharing photos, or a printer that keeps going offline. Your helper can write down the steps to keep next to the device.

## What it doesn't cover

Helpers don't handle passwords, bank or other financial accounts, or cryptocurrency, and they don't use unattended remote-access tools.
