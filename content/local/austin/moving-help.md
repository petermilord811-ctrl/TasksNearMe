Moving help covers the lifting: loading and unloading the truck or container you've arranged, moving furniture between rooms, and packing. Your helper doesn't bring a vehicle, so book your transport first and plan the helper's hours around it.

## Timing in Austin

Summers are long and hot, so start heavy lifting early in the day from May to September, and keep water on hand. Late July and August are busy: many leases turn over and students move in around the University of Texas, so trucks and elevators get booked early. The last weekend of the month is busy all year.

## Apartment moves

Many apartment buildings ask you to reserve the freight elevator or the loading area, and some set moving hours. Check with your building, then put the details in your task: the floor, how far the truck parks from the door, and any stairs.

## How the price works

Moving help is booked by the hour, with a maximum number of hours. You pay for the maximum when you book, and unused time is refunded after the final hours are approved. Pianos, safes and hazardous materials aren't part of moving help.
