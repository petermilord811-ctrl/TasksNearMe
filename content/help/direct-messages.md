You can write to a neighbor or a helper without posting a task. Choose **Message** on a helper's profile or on someone's Neighbors card, or start a new message from **Messages** and find them by their @username.

## How many you can start

The limit is on **new conversations**, counted over any 24 hours:

- **Starter** (no plan): 3 a day. Sparks raise it as you reach each level: 10, 25, 30, 40 and then 60 a day.
- **Plus**: 25 a day, or your level's if that's more.
- **Pro**: 100 a day.

Replies, conversations someone else started, and conversations about a task, an offer or a booking never count. Each conversation you start frees up again 24 hours later. See [pricing](/pricing) for the plans.

## Until they answer

You can send up to 3 messages before the other person answers. Once they answer, keep the conversation going.

## Message requests

When someone you haven't talked to about a task writes to you first, their message waits under **Requests** in Messages, and it doesn't add to your Messages count. Open it to **Accept** it, answer it, **Ignore** it (they aren't told) or **Block** them.

## Keeping track

In Messages and in a conversation's **⋯** menu you can pin up to 3 conversations on top, mute one (no notifications from it) or archive it until the other person writes again. **Search in conversation** finds words in it, and **Translate** shows a message in your language. When someone has written to you, **Suggest replies** offers a few short answers to start from; nothing is sent until you send it.

Someone's @username in a message shows as their name and opens their profile.

## Who can message you

In your account, under **Direct messages**, choose who can start a conversation with you: everyone, only people you've talked to about a task, or nobody. Blocking someone stops their messages at once, and you can report a message that breaks the [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines).

## What stays private

Phone numbers, emails and links in messages stay hidden until you've booked together. Keep the conversation on TasksNearMe so there's a record if you ever need help. More in [staying safe](/help/staying-safe).

## Helpers who share their contact details

A helper can choose, in their account under **Contact details on your profile**, to share their email, their phone number or both. Only neighbors who've earned 2,000 Sparks, the Pillar and Local legend levels, see them, on the helper's profile after choosing **Show**. Bought Sparks don't count toward it. We keep a record of who sees them, and the helper can stop sharing at any time. Book and pay through TasksNearMe all the same: we protect only payments we hold ([terms](/legal/terms#off-platform)).
