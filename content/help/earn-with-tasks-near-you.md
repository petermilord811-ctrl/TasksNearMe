If you're handy, strong or patient with a computer, your neighbors need you. On TasksNearMe you pick the tasks, set your price and keep all of it: there are no service fees.

## Get verified once

Before you send offers, you verify your home address. Services in someone's home also need a cleared background check. Your ID, your phone (if we ask for it) and payouts make you a verified helper, whenever you like. See [getting verified](/help/getting-verified).

If we're not in your city yet, you can sign up and finish your checks now. We'll tell you when your city opens.

## Find work and make offers

Open tasks show what each one pays, when it's needed and the general area. When one fits:

- **Offer your price** and a start time inside the poster's window.
- **Ask a question** if the task isn't clear.
- **Keep to the scope.** Anything extra needs an agreed change first.

## Do the work, get paid

After the task, you add what's needed (photos, hours) and the poster approves it. If they don't respond, it's approved automatically after 72 hours. In each offer you choose how you're paid: directly by the poster after the task, or upfront, held by TasksNearMe until the poster approves. Either way you earn the whole price, with no helper fee. See [when do I get paid](/help/when-do-i-get-paid).

## Build your reputation

Good reviews and repeat bookings bring more work your way. Clients can save you and book you again.
