If you're handy, strong or patient with a computer, your neighbors need you. On TasksNearMe you pick the tasks, set your price and get paid through the app.

## Get verified once

Before you send offers, you verify your phone, a government ID with a live selfie, and your home address. Services in someone's home also need a cleared background check. See [getting verified](/help/getting-verified).

If we're not in your city yet, you can sign up and finish your checks now. We'll tell you when your city opens.

## Find work and make offers

Open tasks show what each one pays, when it's needed and the general area. When one fits:

- **Offer your price** and a start time inside the poster's window.
- **Ask a question** if the task isn't clear.
- **Keep to the scope.** Anything extra needs an agreed change first.

## Do the work, get paid

After the task, you add what's needed (photos, hours) and the poster approves it. If they don't respond, it's approved automatically after 72 hours. Your earnings are the task price minus a 10% helper fee, and you see that number before you offer. See [when do I get paid](/help/when-do-i-get-paid).

## Build your reputation

Good reviews and repeat bookings bring more work your way. Clients can save you and book you again.
