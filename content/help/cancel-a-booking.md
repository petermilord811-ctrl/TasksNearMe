Plans change. Here is what canceling costs.

## If you cancel

- **24 hours or more before the start:** free. You get everything back.
- **Less than 24 hours before:** a late fee of 20% of the task price (at least $10.00, never more than the task price) goes to your helper. You get the rest back.

## If your helper cancels

You get everything back, and you can book someone else.

## If we cancel

If our support team has to cancel a booking, for example over a safety concern, you get a full refund.

## No-shows and access problems

If you're not there or your helper can't get in, it counts as a late cancellation. If your helper doesn't show up, you can report it 30 minutes after the start time and get a full refund.

The full rules are in the [cancellation policy](/legal/cancellation-policy).
