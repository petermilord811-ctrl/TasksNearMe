Plans change. Here is what canceling costs. Money only moves through TasksNearMe when you pay upfront, so the refunds below are for those bookings.

## If you cancel

- **24 hours or more before the start:** free. You get everything back, including the hold fee.
- **Less than 24 hours before:** a late fee of 20% of the task price (at least $10.00, never more than the task price) goes to your helper. You get the rest back, including the hold fee.
- **If you pay your helper directly:** nothing was charged here, so there's no late fee and nothing to refund. Please still cancel as early as you can: your helper set the time aside for you.

## If your helper cancels

You get back everything you paid upfront, and you can book someone else.

## If we cancel

If our support team has to cancel a booking, for example over a safety concern, you get a full refund of anything you paid upfront.

## No-shows and access problems

If you're not there or your helper can't get in, it counts as a late cancellation. If your helper doesn't show up, you can report it 30 minutes after the start time, and get a full refund if you paid upfront.

The full rules are in the [cancellation policy](/legal/cancellation-policy).
