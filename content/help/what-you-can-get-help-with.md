TasksNearMe covers five everyday services. Knowing what each includes helps you post a task helpers can say yes to.

## Furniture assembly

Flat-pack furniture such as beds, desks and shelves, priced per item. Anti-tip anchoring can be part of the task if you agree on it. Not included: electrical, gas or plumbing work, and TV mounting.

## Moving help

Loading and unloading, moving furniture within or between rooms, and packing, by the hour. You arrange the truck: helpers don't transport your things in their own vehicle. Not included: pianos, safes or hazardous materials.

## Yard cleanup

Raking and bagging leaves, weeding, ground-level debris pickup and light hand pruning. Not included: pesticides, herbicides or irrigation repair, ladders, chainsaws or tree work, and hauling debris away.

## Light cleaning

Standard home cleaning, kitchens and bathrooms, and a light tidy. Describe the rooms, supplies and any pets. Not included: biohazards or mold, post-construction cleaning, and exterior windows above the ground floor.

## Computer and phone help

Device setup, Wi-Fi and printers, backups and basic cleanup, and patient how-to help, at home or in a guided screen-sharing session. A helper never handles your passwords or financial accounts, and unattended remote access isn't allowed.
