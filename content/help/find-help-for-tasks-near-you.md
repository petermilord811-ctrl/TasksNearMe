Furniture to put together, a truck to load, leaves to bag: most everyday tasks go faster with one more pair of hands. This is how to find the right person for yours.

## Describe the task well

The clearer the task, the better the offers.

- **Say what needs doing,** item by item: "a desk and two bookshelves, all flat-pack", not "some furniture".
- **Add photos.** They answer most questions before anyone asks.
- **Give the access details:** stairs, parking, a gate code to share after booking.
- **Pick a time window** that works for you. Helpers offer a start time inside it.

You only share your general area when you post. See [who sees my address](/help/who-sees-my-address).

## Compare offers

Helpers nearby send offers with their price and a start time. For each one, look at:

- **Their checks.** Every helper verifies a government ID with a live selfie and their home address before they can send offers. Services in your home also need a cleared background check.
- **Reviews and completed tasks** from people who booked them.
- **The price, what it covers and how to pay.** There are no service fees. Each offer says whether you pay the helper directly after the task, or upfront, held by TasksNearMe for a small hold fee, and you see your total before you book.

## Book through TasksNearMe

When you book, the price is agreed, and your helper gets your exact address only then. If you pay directly, pay once the work is done, never before. Only payments TasksNearMe holds can be refunded through us, so if you'd like that protection, choose an offer paid upfront or ask your helper for one. See [when am I charged](/help/when-am-i-charged).

## After the task

You approve the work, or report a problem if something isn't right. If you're paying directly, this is when you pay your helper. Then you can review your helper and book them again.

TasksNearMe opens city by city. See [where we are](/cities), or read [how it works](/how-it-works).
