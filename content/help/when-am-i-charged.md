It depends on how your helper asked to be paid. Each offer shows it before you book, and there are no service fees either way.

## Paying your helper directly

Nothing is charged on TasksNearMe, and you don't need a card on file. After the task, you pay your helper the agreed price directly, in cash, with a payment app or however you agree. Tips go to your helper directly too.

## Paying upfront

When a helper asks to be paid upfront, we charge your card when you book and hold the money until you say the task is done. Then it goes to your helper. If you don't respond, it goes to them automatically 72 hours after they mark the task done.

## What you pay upfront

- The task price you agreed with your helper.
- A hold fee of 5% of the task price, at least $1.00. With a Pro plan there's no hold fee.
- Any expense allowance you agreed on, for example for parts. Unused allowance is refunded.

## Hourly tasks

When you pay upfront for an hourly task, we charge the hourly rate times the hour cap when you book. After the task you approve the hours worked, and we refund the unused hours and their share of the hold fee. If you pay directly, you pay your helper for the hours worked.

## Bookings further ahead

- **More than 14 days ahead, paid upfront**: nothing is charged when you book. We charge your card 3 days before the start. If that doesn't go through, you can pay on the booking page until 24 hours before, or the booking is canceled with no fees.
- **Regular visits**: each visit is charged 2 days before it. If that doesn't go through, you can pay on the booking page until 12 hours before, or that visit is skipped with no fees.

## Invitations

When you invite a helper directly, we place a hold on your card. The hold becomes a charge only if the helper accepts within 48 hours. Otherwise it's released.

If we ever pause new bookings for a short while, for example during a payment outage, nothing is charged for new bookings, and the ones you already have go ahead.

> [!info] Every example, with exact numbers, is on our [pricing page](/pricing).

Card payments are processed by [Stripe](https://stripe.com).
