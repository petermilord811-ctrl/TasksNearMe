If something went wrong, tell us before you approve the task, **within 72 hours** of your helper marking it done. If TasksNearMe holds the payment, it's paused while we look into it.

## How to report

1. Open the booking and choose **Report a problem**.
2. Tell us what happened, and add photos if you can.
3. We may ask you and your helper a few questions.

## What can happen

- Your helper can offer to come back and fix it.
- If we hold the payment, you and your helper can agree on a lower price, and we refund the difference.
- If the work wasn't done, we refund a held payment in full.
- If you paid your helper directly, we can't refund that money. We can still help you both sort it out, and act on what you report, up to removing someone from TasksNearMe.
- Most reports are resolved within 3 business days.

> [!warning] If anyone is in danger, call 911 first. Then report it to us.
