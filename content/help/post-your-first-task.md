Posting is free. You only pay when you book a helper.

## What to include

- **What needs doing**, in a sentence or two. "Assemble one flat-pack desk, about 40 parts" works better than "furniture help".
- **When**: a date and a time window, or any time that day.
- **Where**: helpers see only your neighborhood until you book someone.
- **Your budget**: a fixed price, or an hourly rate with a cap on hours.
- **Photos** help helpers price the job. Only signed-in helpers see them.

## What happens next

1. Helpers who cover your area get an alert and send offers.
2. Compare offers, profiles, and reviews. You can message a helper before you book.
3. Book the helper you want. You see the full total before you confirm.

> [!info] If we ask for your phone number before you post, we text you a code once to confirm it.

See [how it works](/how-it-works) for the whole journey.
