On TasksNearMe you agree on the price with your helper before you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

## Fixed price or by the hour

- **A fixed price** suits tasks you can describe completely: assembling a desk, a standard clean of a small apartment. Many services are priced per item or per task.
- **An hourly rate** suits work whose length is hard to know in advance, like moving help. You agree on a rate and a maximum number of hours. You pay for that maximum when you book, and unused time is refunded after the final hours are approved.

Each city's service pages show the typical price range there, and its price guides work out what you'd pay at each end.

## What changes the price

- **Size and amount:** the number of items, rooms or bags.
- **Access:** stairs, a long walk from parking, tight spaces.
- **Timing:** the same day or a short window costs more than a flexible one.
- **What's included:** tools, supplies, haul-away. Say what you have and what you need.

## The fees

You see every fee before you pay:

- **Posters** pay a service fee on top of the task price, today 10% (at least $3.00).
- **Helpers** pay 10% of the task price, so they know what they earn before they offer.

Canceling is free until 24 hours before the start. See [pricing](/pricing) for the current fees and [canceling a booking](/help/cancel-a-booking) for the rest.
