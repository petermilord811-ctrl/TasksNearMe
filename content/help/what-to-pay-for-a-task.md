On TasksNearMe you agree on the price with your helper before you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

## Fixed price or by the hour

- **A fixed price** suits tasks you can describe completely: assembling a desk, a standard clean of a small apartment. Many services are priced per item or per task.
- **An hourly rate** suits work whose length is hard to know in advance, like moving help. You agree on a rate and a maximum number of hours. If you pay your helper directly, you pay for the hours worked after the task. If you pay upfront, you pay for that maximum when you book, and unused time is refunded after the final hours are approved.

Each city's service pages show the typical price range there, and its price guides work out what you'd pay at each end.

## What changes the price

- **Size and amount:** the number of items, rooms or bags.
- **Access:** stairs, a long walk from parking, tight spaces.
- **Timing:** the same day or a short window costs more than a flexible one.
- **What's included:** tools, supplies, haul-away. Say what you have and what you need.

## No service fees

Posting, offers, messages and booking are free, for you and for your helper. Each offer says how the helper wants to be paid:

- **Directly:** you pay your helper the agreed price after the task, in cash, with a payment app or however you agree. There's no fee, and the money doesn't go through TasksNearMe.
- **Upfront, held by TasksNearMe:** you pay when you book, and we hold the money until you say the task is done. You pay a hold fee, today 5% of the task price (at least $1.00), or none with a Pro plan. Your helper gets the whole price.

For a $60.00 desk, paying directly costs $60.00. Paying upfront costs $63.00 ($60.00 plus a $3.00 hold fee). Your helper gets $60.00 either way.

Canceling a booking paid upfront is free until 24 hours before the start. See [pricing](/pricing) for the plans and examples and [canceling a booking](/help/cancel-a-booking) for the rest.
