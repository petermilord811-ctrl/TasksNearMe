Before you can send offers, we check a few things. Most people finish in about 10 minutes.

## The steps

1. **Your profile**: your services, prices, and the area you cover.
2. **ID check**, in two parts: photos of your government ID and a live selfie, which our team compares (usually within a day), then a quick double check with our ID partner. Clients then see an ID-verified badge with the date. Your photos are stored encrypted and deleted no later than 30 days after our review.
3. **Payout setup**: your bank details, held by our payment provider, not by us.
4. **Background check**: only for in-home categories, such as furniture assembly and light cleaning.

> [!info] Badges show what was checked and when. They don't guarantee work quality; that's what reviews are for.
