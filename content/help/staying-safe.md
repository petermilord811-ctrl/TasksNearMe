Most tasks go smoothly. These habits help keep it that way.

## For everyone

- Keep your messages and bookings in TasksNearMe, so what you agreed is on record.
- Paying your helper directly? Pay once the work is done, never in advance to someone you haven't met. Only payments TasksNearMe holds are protected by us, so if you want that protection, ask for a held payment.
- Check the profile: badges, reviews, and how long they've been a member.
- Trust your instincts. You can cancel and report anything that feels wrong.

## For helpers

- Tell someone where you're going if you work alone.
- Don't do anything outside the agreed task, such as handling passwords or banking, or anything on the prohibited list.

## If something goes wrong

- During a booking, choose **Report a safety concern** on the booking page. It reaches our safety team right away, at any hour.
- From a conversation, you can report or block someone. Blocked people can't message, offer, or invite each other.
- You can also report a task, a profile, or a review from its page.

> [!warning] In an emergency, call 911 first.

See the [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) and the [prohibited tasks](/legal/prohibited-tasks).
