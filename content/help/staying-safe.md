Most tasks go smoothly. These habits help keep it that way.

## For everyone

- Keep messages and payments in TasksNearMe. Payments outside the app aren't covered if something goes wrong.
- Check the profile: badges, reviews, and how long they've been a member.
- Trust your instincts. You can cancel and report anything that feels wrong.

## For helpers

- Tell someone where you're going if you work alone.
- Don't do anything outside the agreed task, such as handling passwords or banking, or anything on the prohibited list.

> [!warning] In an emergency, call 911 first.

See the [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) and the [prohibited tasks](/legal/prohibited-tasks).
