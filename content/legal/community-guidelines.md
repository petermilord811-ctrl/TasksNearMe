> [!warning] This is a working draft for review. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not in effect.

## 1. Be respectful {#respect}
No harassment, discrimination, or threats, in messages or in person.

## 2. Be honest {#honest}
Describe tasks and services accurately. Leave reviews that reflect what happened. Never offer or ask for anything in exchange for a review.

## 3. Keep it safe {#safe}
Follow the [prohibited tasks](/legal/prohibited-tasks) list. In an emergency, call 911 first, then report it to us.

## 4. Keep it on TasksNearMe {#on-platform}
Book and pay in the app, so both of you are covered.

## 5. Reporting {#reporting}
Report anything that breaks these guidelines from the booking, the message, or the profile. Breaking them can lead to warnings, suspension, or removal.
