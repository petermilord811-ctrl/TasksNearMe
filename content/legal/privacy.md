## 1. What we collect {#collect}
- **Account details:** name, email, and phone number.
- **Task and booking details:** descriptions, photos, messages, addresses, and reviews.
- **Verification results** for helpers: whether an ID or background check passed, and when.
- **ID photos** for a helper's ID check: photos of the ID and a selfie, which our trust and safety team compares before our ID partner double-checks them. They're kept encrypted until both checks are finished and deleted no later than 30 days after our decision. While our team reviews, software on our own servers can read the barcode on the back of a license or state ID and show them what it says; that isn't stored. From the ID itself we keep only yes-or-no answers (the name matches, the address matches, 18 or older, not expired) and its expiry month and year, never its number or the birth date. We also keep a one-way code made from the ID's number, issuing state and birth date, which can't be turned back into them, only to notice the same ID on two accounts; it's deleted with your account, unless the account was banned for breaking our rules: then we keep only the one-way code, so the same person can't sign up again. We remind you 30 days before the ID's expiry month ends, so you can verify a current one.
- **Proof of address** for a helper's home address check: a recent document with your name and address, which our trust and safety team checks. The document is deleted 30 days after our decision, and at once if you delete your account. We keep the result, its dates and your address, encrypted.
- **Payment records** from our payment provider. We never see full card or bank numbers.
- **Device and usage data:** the browsers you're signed in on (with their IP address), the passkeys you add, and the devices that get your notifications, to keep the service secure and working.

## 2. How we use it {#use}
To run bookings and payments, verify helpers, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, and improve the service.

## 3. Who sees what {#sharing}
- Other people see your first name and last initial, never your last name.
- Your exact address goes only to the helper you book, after the booking is confirmed, and is hidden again 48 hours after the task. We delete it 180 days after the last booking there, or after the task closes if nobody booked it.
- We share data with service providers that help us run TasksNearMe (payments, identity checks, email and text messages, finding roughly where an IP address is, hosting), under contracts that limit their use.
- When you ask for help writing (a task description, your profile or an offer message) or search with AI, what you typed goes to the AI service that writes the answer, with phone numbers, emails, links and street addresses taken out first. It never gets your ID or proof of address: software on our own servers reads those.

## 4. Your choices {#choices}
You can update your details, download your data, or ask us to delete your account from your account settings.

- **A copy of your data:** a zip file with everything linked to your account and the photos you added, ready to download for 7 days. Identity documents aren't included: we delete them within 30 days of our decision.
- **Deleting your account:** we wait 7 days, so you can change your mind, and until nothing is open: a booking, a support case, or money on its way to you. Then we delete your name, contact details, sign-ins, passkeys, devices, saved searches, your profile, task and work photos, and your addresses.
- **Photos and addresses while you have an account:** task photos are deleted 2 years after the task closes, and exact addresses as in section 3.

What we keep after you delete your account, and for how long:
- **Payment, payout and tax records:** 7 years, as the law requires.
- **Bookings, and reviews you wrote:** as records, shown under "Deleted account".
- **Messages you sent:** with the people you sent them to, until 2 years after the conversation's last message.
- **Completion photos and receipts:** 2 years after the booking.
- **A helper's verified home address:** encrypted, until 2 years after your last booking, or longer while a case about one is open. If you had no bookings, it's deleted with your account.
- **Verification results and their dates.** Background check reports stay with our background check provider, as federal law (the FCRA) requires.
- **The record of changes to your account (our audit log):** 7 years, without your contact details.
- **If the account was banned for breaking our rules:** the one-way ID code and the verified home address, so the same person can't sign up again.
