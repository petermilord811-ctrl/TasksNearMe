## 1. What we collect {#collect}
- **Account details:** name, email, and phone number.
- **Task and booking details:** descriptions, photos, messages, addresses, and reviews.
- **Verification results** for helpers: whether an ID or background check passed, and when.
- **ID photos** for a helper's ID check: photos of the ID and a selfie. Software on our own servers checks the ID and its barcode and compares the face in the selfie with the photo on the ID (face geometry, a biometric identifier), and our trust and safety team reviews them; where an ID partner double-checks the ID, it does the same. We keep the photos and the details read from the ID (such as the name, birth date, ID number, address and dates on it) encrypted, use them only to verify your identity, review your check and prevent fraud, and delete them no later than one year after our decision, at once if you delete your account. We also keep a one-way code made from the ID's number, issuing state and birth date, which can't be turned back into them, only to notice the same ID on two accounts; it's deleted with your account, unless the account was banned for breaking our rules: then we keep only the one-way code, so the same person can't sign up again. We remind you 30 days before the ID's expiry month ends, so you can verify a current one.
- **Proof of address** for a helper's home address check: a recent document with your name and address, which our trust and safety team checks. We keep the document while your account is open, so our team can compare it with a later proof if one is needed, and delete it at once if you delete your account. We keep the result, its dates and your address, encrypted.
- **Payment records** from our payment provider. We never see full card or bank numbers.
- **Device and usage data:** the browsers you're signed in on (with their IP address), a record of each sign-in (its IP address, browser and how you signed in) kept for one year, the passkeys you add, and the devices that get your notifications, to keep the service secure and working and to prevent fraud.

## 2. How we use it {#use}
To run bookings and payments, verify helpers, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, and improve the service.

## 3. Who sees what {#sharing}
- Other people see your first name and last initial, never your last name.
- If you're a helper and choose to share your email or phone number on your profile (account settings, **Contact details on your profile**; off unless you turn it on), members who've earned 2,000 Sparks can see them there. We record each time someone does, and you can stop sharing at any time; people who already saw them may have kept them.
- Your exact address goes only to the helper you book, after the booking is confirmed, and is hidden again 48 hours after the task. We delete it 180 days after the last booking there, or after the task closes if nobody booked it.
- We share data with service providers that help us run TasksNearMe (payments, identity checks, email and text messages, hosting), under contracts that limit their use.
- When our staff review an account, ipinfo.io receives the IP addresses it used (to sign in, accept an agreement or take another action), and nothing else, and tells us roughly where each one is and what kind of network it's on. We don't store its answers. With the results of the account's checks, they make a fraud risk score that only our staff see; it helps them decide what to look at, and it never decides anything on its own.
- When you ask for help writing (a task description, your profile, an offer message, a review, a Neighbors post or an answer), check a task's price, make a checklist for a booking, search with AI, or ask the support assistant in Messages, what you typed goes to the AI service that writes the answer (for a checklist, the booking's details as you see them), with phone numbers, emails, links and street addresses taken out first. To sum up what neighbors say on a helper's profile, the words of their published reviews go to it the same way, without the reviewers' names. Our support team can ask it to summarize a support case they're handling, from what their case page shows. It never gets your ID or proof of address: software on our own servers reads those. We don't keep your chat with the support assistant unless you ask to talk to a person; then it goes to our support team with your request.

## 4. Your choices {#choices}
You can update your details, download your data, or ask us to delete your account from your account settings.

- **A copy of your data:** a zip file with everything linked to your account and the photos you added, ready to download for 7 days. ID photos and proofs of address aren't included.
- **Deleting your account:** we wait 7 days, so you can change your mind, and until nothing is open: a booking, a support case, or money on its way to you. Then we delete your name, contact details, sign-ins, passkeys, devices, saved searches, your profile, task and work photos, and your addresses.
- **Photos and addresses while you have an account:** task photos are deleted 2 years after the task closes, and exact addresses as in section 3.

What we keep after you delete your account, and for how long:
- **Payment, payout and tax records:** 7 years, as the law requires.
- **Bookings, and reviews you wrote:** as records, shown under "Deleted account".
- **Messages you sent:** with the people you sent them to, until 2 years after the conversation's last message. Messages in a building or street group, until 2 years after the group's last message.
- **The earlier wording of a post or reply you edited:** 2 years after the edit, so a report can be read as it was written.
- **Thank-you notes you wrote:** as records, like reviews, shown under "Deleted account" or by neighborhood alone. You can delete a note at any time before then.
- **Completion photos and receipts:** 2 years after the booking.
- **A helper's verified home address:** encrypted, until 2 years after your last booking, or longer while a case about one is open. If you had no bookings, it's deleted with your account.
- **Verification results and their dates.** Background check reports stay with our background check provider, as federal law (the FCRA) requires.
- **The record of changes to your account (our audit log):** 7 years, without your contact details.
- **If the account was banned for breaking our rules:** the one-way ID code and the verified home address, so the same person can't sign up again.
