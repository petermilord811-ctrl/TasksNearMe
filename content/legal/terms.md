## 1. Who we are {#who-we-are}
TasksNearMe runs a marketplace where people who need help ("posters") and people who offer help ("helpers") find each other. Helpers are independent. They choose which tasks to take and how to do them. We are not a party to the agreement between a poster and a helper, and we don't employ helpers.

## 2. Your account {#account}
- You must be 18 or older.
- One person, one account. Keep your sign-in codes to yourself.
- Posting, making offers, and booking need a verified phone number.
- Give accurate information, and keep it up to date.

## 3. Tasks and bookings {#bookings}
A booking is an agreement between a poster and a helper on the task, time, and price shown at confirmation. Changes need both people to agree in the app. Tasks must follow the [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) and must not be on the [prohibited list](/legal/prohibited-tasks).

## 4. Payments {#payments}
We don't charge service fees. Posting, making offers, messaging, and booking are free for posters and helpers. Each offer states how the helper will be paid:

- **Paid directly** (the default): the poster pays the helper directly after the task, by any method they agree on. That payment is between the poster and the helper. We don't process, hold, or refund it, and we charge no fee on it.
- **Paid upfront and held by TasksNearMe**: if the helper asks to be paid upfront, the poster pays when they book: the task price, a hold fee, and any agreed expense allowance. We hold the money as a neutral third party and release the task price to the helper when the poster confirms the task is done, or automatically 72 hours after the helper marks it done if the poster doesn't respond. The helper receives the whole task price, with no fee deducted. Members of the Plus plan pay no hold fee.

The hold fee and worked examples are on the [pricing page](/pricing). Held payments are processed by our payment provider, Stripe.

## 5. Cancellations and problems {#cancellations}
Cancellations follow the [cancellation policy](/legal/cancellation-policy). On a held booking, a problem reported within 72 hours of completion pauses the helper's payment while we review it. We can refund or settle only money we hold. For a booking paid directly, we can help the poster and the helper resolve a problem, act on reports, and suspend or remove accounts, but we can't refund money paid directly.

## 6. Paying outside TasksNearMe {#off-platform}
Book tasks you find on TasksNearMe through TasksNearMe, so the agreed terms, your messages, and reviews stay on record. A poster may pay a helper directly, outside TasksNearMe. We protect only payments we hold, and we can't refund or recover money paid directly. Never pay someone you haven't met before the work is done; if you want your payment protected, choose a held payment.

## 7. Liability {#liability}
To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the fees we received for the booking concerned. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under applicable consumer law.

## 8. Changes and contact {#changes}
We'll tell you before material changes take effect. Questions? Visit the [help center](/help).
