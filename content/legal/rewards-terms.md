## 1. Welcome reward {#welcome}
A $50 gift card for our first 100 verified helpers in a market, after your first completed task of $40 or more paid through TasksNearMe (paid upfront and held; a task paid directly doesn't count) within 60 days of verification. One welcome reward per person.

## 2. Repeat-client reward {#repeat}
A $10 gift card when you complete your 3rd booking paid through TasksNearMe with the same client within 90 days. Each booking must be $25 or more. Once per helper and client pair.

## 3. Unlocking and expiry {#timing}
Rewards show as pending first. They unlock 7 days after the qualifying task is paid, if there's no refund, dispute, or risk review. Redeem within 90 days of unlocking.

## 4. Fair play {#fair-play}
Rewards never depend on reviews or ratings. We review rewards for abuse, such as linked accounts, and may void rewards that don't qualify. Rewards are gift cards, not wages, and may be taxable income.

## 5. Changes {#changes}
We may change or end reward programs. Rewards you've already earned keep the terms they were earned under.
