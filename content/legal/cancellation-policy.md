> [!warning] This is a working draft for review. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not in effect.

## 1. Poster cancels early {#early}
Canceling 24 hours or more before the start is free. You get everything back.

## 2. Poster cancels late {#late}
Canceling less than 24 hours before the start costs a late fee of 20% of the task price, at least $10.00 and never more than the task price. The whole fee goes to the helper. You get the rest back.

## 3. Helper cancels {#helper}
If the helper cancels or doesn't show up, the poster gets everything back.

## 4. No-shows and access {#no-show}
If the poster isn't there or the helper can't get in at the agreed time, it counts as a late cancellation. A poster can report a helper no-show 30 minutes after the start time.

## 5. Example {#example}
On a $60.00 task (you paid $66.00): canceling at least 24 hours before refunds $66.00. Canceling later refunds $54.00, and your helper receives $12.00. See the [pricing page](/pricing).
