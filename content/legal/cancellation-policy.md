## 1. Poster cancels early {#early}
Canceling 24 hours or more before the start is free. On a booking paid upfront and held by TasksNearMe, you get everything back, including the hold fee.

## 2. Poster cancels late {#late}
On a held booking, canceling less than 24 hours before the start costs a late fee of 20% of the task price, at least $10.00 and never more than the task price. The whole fee goes to the helper. You get the rest back, including the hold fee. On a booking paid directly, no money moves through TasksNearMe, so there's no late fee and nothing to refund. We still ask you to cancel as early as you can, and repeated late cancellations can limit an account under our Terms.

## 3. Helper cancels {#helper}
If the helper cancels or doesn't show up, the poster gets back everything paid upfront for the booking, including the hold fee.

## 4. No-shows and access {#no-show}
If the poster isn't there or the helper can't get in at the agreed time, it counts as a late cancellation. A poster can report a helper no-show 30 minutes after the start time.

## 5. Example {#example}
On a $60.00 task paid upfront (you paid $63.00, including the $3.00 hold fee): canceling at least 24 hours before refunds $63.00. Canceling later refunds $51.00, and your helper receives $12.00. Paid directly, nothing was charged, so nothing is refunded. See the [pricing page](/pricing).
