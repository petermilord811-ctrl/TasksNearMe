TasksNearMe connects people in Gainesville with verified neighbors for everyday tasks: [furniture assembly](/gainesville/furniture-assembly), [moving help](/gainesville/moving-help), [yard cleanup](/gainesville/yard-cleanup), [light cleaning](/gainesville/light-cleaning), and [computer and phone help](/gainesville/computer-help). You describe what you need, helpers nearby send offers with their price, and you choose the one that fits.

## Where it works

The service area is the city and the rest of Alachua County within about 15 miles of downtown. It runs from University Heights and Midtown near campus to Duckpond, Millhopper, Thornebrook, Haile Plantation, and the neighborhoods east and south of downtown. Your exact address stays private: helpers see only the general area while they make offers, and only the helper you book sees the address.

## The Gainesville year

- **August, December, and May:** UF's move weeks. Moving help, furniture builds, and move-out cleaning book up fast, so post early if you can.
- **Late winter and spring:** live oaks and laurel oaks drop their old leaves, and pollen coats porches and sills. Yard cleanups and a wipe-down near the doors are common.
- **June to September:** heat and afternoon thunderstorms. Outdoor work goes best early in the day.
- **Hurricane season, June to November:** storms bring down branches and leaves. Yard cleanup covers debris at ground level; tree work and hauling aren't part of it.

## Prices and payment

You and your helper agree on the price before you book: a fixed price for the job, or an hourly rate with the most hours you want to pay for. There are no service fees: you pay your helper directly once the task is done, in cash or with a payment app. If a helper asks to be paid upfront, your card is charged when you book, plus a small hold fee, and TasksNearMe holds the money until you say the task is done. Each service has a cost guide for Gainesville, such as [what furniture assembly costs](/gainesville/furniture-assembly/cost).

## Who you meet

Before sending offers, every helper verifies their phone, an official ID with a live selfie, and their home address. Services inside a home also need a background check. Read more about [trust and safety](/trust-and-safety), or see [how it works](/how-it-works).
